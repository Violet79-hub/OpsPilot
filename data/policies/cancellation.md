# 08 Subscription Cancellation Policy

## 7.1 Subscription refunds

Subscription orders may receive a full refund within 7 days of purchase, provided the subscription is unused. Used subscriptions and requests after 7 days are non-refundable; support can arrange cancellation at the end of the billing period. Gold extensions apply only to physical products.

## Scope and recordkeeping

This is a synthetic Northstar Commerce policy, effective 2026-10-01. Policy owner: Operations. Applies only to this demonstration dataset. Decisions must retain order evidence, retrieved policy sections and human review history. A recommendation is not a payment transaction.
