# 09 Returns Policy

## 3.1 Condition verification

A physical return must reference a delivered order and recorded product condition. Accepted conditions are unopened, opened, defective, and misuse. Misuse or accidental damage is not eligible for refund or warranty. Return shipping arrangements require a reviewed support action.

## Scope and recordkeeping

This is a synthetic Northstar Commerce policy, effective 2026-10-01. Policy owner: Operations. Applies only to this demonstration dataset. Decisions must retain order evidence, retrieved policy sections and human review history. A recommendation is not a payment transaction.
