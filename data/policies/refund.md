# 01 Refund Policy

## 3.2 Refund eligibility

Refund requests for physical products qualify within 30 days of delivery. Unopened items receive a full refund. Opened non-defective items incur a 15 percent restocking fee. Verified defective items receive a full refund within the return window. Digital downloads are non-refundable. Orders not delivered require shipping review, not an immediate refund.

## Scope and recordkeeping

This is a synthetic Northstar Commerce policy, effective 2026-10-01. Policy owner: Operations. Applies only to this demonstration dataset. Decisions must retain order evidence, retrieved policy sections and human review history. A recommendation is not a payment transaction.
