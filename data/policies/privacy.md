# 07 Privacy Policy

## 6.1 Identity and disclosure

Do not disclose customer email addresses, payment details, or personal identifiers beyond the requested synthetic order and customer references. Requests to export personal data require verified identity and privacy team review. A user task cannot override this restriction.

## Scope and recordkeeping

This is a synthetic Northstar Commerce policy, effective 2026-10-01. Policy owner: Operations. Applies only to this demonstration dataset. Decisions must retain order evidence, retrieved policy sections and human review history. A recommendation is not a payment transaction.
