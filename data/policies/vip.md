# 04 VIP Customer Policy

## 2.2 Gold return extension

Gold tier customers have a 45 day return window for physical products, measured from delivery. Standard and Silver customers have 30 days. Gold status does not override fraud holds, digital download exclusions, or privacy requirements. Non-defective opened goods still incur the restocking fee.

This is a synthetic Northstar Commerce policy, effective 2026-10-01. Policy owner: Operations. Applies only to this demonstration dataset. Decisions must retain order evidence, retrieved policy sections and human review history. A recommendation is not a payment transaction.
