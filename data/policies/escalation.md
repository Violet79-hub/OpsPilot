# 06 Escalation Guide

## 5.1 Manual review

Refunds above 1500 USD require specialist review even if otherwise eligible. A high risk customer or suspected fraud requires escalation and prohibits an automated refund recommendation. Human approval creates an internal support action only; this demo has no payment gateway.

## Scope and recordkeeping

This is a synthetic Northstar Commerce policy, effective 2026-10-01. Policy owner: Operations. Applies only to this demonstration dataset. Decisions must retain order evidence, retrieved policy sections and human review history. A recommendation is not a payment transaction.
